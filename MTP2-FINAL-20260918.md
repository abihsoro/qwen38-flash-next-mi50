## Results

| Prompt tokens | Median effective PP | Median first-token latency | Median decode | Decode range |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1,000 | 641.9 tok/s | 1.558 s | 60.34 tok/s | 60.12–60.35 tok/s |
| 10,000 | 806.9 tok/s | 12.393 s | 61.22 tok/s | 39.02–65.35 tok/s |
| 30,000 | 805.1 tok/s | 37.263 s | 57.34 tok/s | 57.00–61.83 tok/s |

Each row is the median of **three scored requests**, after two complete warmup requests at that length. Every request generated exactly 128 tokens; prompt counts are exactly 1,000, 10,000 and 30,000, verified from returned usage. There was one active request at a time. The prompt was the existing synthetic hash-table prose corpus, repeated and truncated to the target token count, with the same instruction suffix. These are throughput measurements, not long-context comprehension tests or a representative workload mix.

Every warmup and scored request used a unique cache salt; all reported zero cached prompt tokens. The runtime and PLE sidecar were warm before benchmarking. Effective PP means prompt tokens divided by server time to first token, including first-token work. Decode excludes that interval and subtracts the entire first streamed token burst. Raw server metrics, client timings, burst sizes, token IDs, output hashes and GPU snapshots are retained.

Prefill was consistent: PP ranges were 640.0–642.2, 805.5–808.1 and 796.4–806.1 tok/s at 1K, 10K and 30K respectively. Decode at 10K varied substantially; its median must be read alongside the range. Client measurements confirm the variation. Temperature-zero continuations matched across all three 1K trials, but differed across the three 10K and 30K trials; these runs do not establish deterministic output or equivalence to MTP0. No cause for the long-context variation was established in this measurement pass.

The post-benchmark answer smoke check passed 11/12, matching the earlier reference and optimized configurations. The same packing question starts with an incorrect answer despite deriving the correct lower bound later. This is a small regression check, not a general quality evaluation. Health returned HTTP 200 after the benchmark; the serving process remains active.
